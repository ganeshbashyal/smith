# Commodity pack v1

The first pack covers gold, silver, copper, iron ore and lithium, with AUD/USD, USD strength, VIX and the ASX 200 as context.

The daily job should append market observations and calculate returns, RSI, moving averages, volatility, drawdown and relative performance. It should not ask Llama to analyse every bar. Llama is reserved for regime changes, unusual volatility, large cross-asset divergences and new supply/demand evidence.

Future evidence layers: real yields, central-bank gold demand, inventories, mine supply, smelter/refinery capacity, China demand, futures curves, producer cost curves and ASX company exposure.

Single-company research

Reports and progress are saved locally and accumulate by run. A bare ticker is treated as an ASX candidate. Live data is retrieved only when MR_SMITH_EXTERNAL_FEEDS=1 is enabled.










Batch opportunity research


Runs one company at a time, saves each data report and local Llama analysis, records any company-specific failure, and continues through the entire queue. Completed companies are skipped if a batch is resumed. This does not load or change portfolio holdings in SQLite.

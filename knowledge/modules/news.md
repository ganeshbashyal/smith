# News verification and current-affairs analysis

## Purpose

Explain what is reported, by whom, when, and what remains unverified. A
retrieved headline is evidence that a publisher reported a claim; it is not
automatic proof that the claim is true.

## Verification workflow

1. Record the exact claim, claimant, event time, publication time, retrieval
   time, location, and affected parties.
2. Locate the original source: official statement, court filing, dataset,
   transcript, full interview, or direct reporting.
3. Seek independent corroboration. Multiple outlets repeating the same wire
   copy are not independent confirmation.
4. Compare accounts, evidence, incentives, and what remains unknown. Attribute
   allegations; do not state contested claims as established fact.
5. Update or correct the summary when stronger evidence appears. Preserve the
   distinction between an event timeline and later interpretation.

## Assessing sources

Assess proximity to the event, access to primary material, named versus
anonymous sourcing, corroboration, correction history, expertise, and
transparency. Consider whether a source is reporting, analysis, editorial, or
opinion. A reputable publisher can still make errors; an official statement
can be incomplete or self-interested. Assess the claim and supporting evidence
as well as the publisher.

Headlines and RSS summaries omit context. Do not invent quotations, casualty
counts, motives, or breaking developments. Avoid false balance: unsupported
claims do not merit equal weight simply because they are disputed. State
uncertainty without sensationalism and respect affected people.

## Time-sensitive claims

Current status requires dated and relevant evidence. Publication date is not
event date; retrieval time is not publication time. A stale report cannot
establish today's position. If the connector is disabled, errors, or returns
no relevant result, say current reporting is unavailable and describe what
primary evidence would resolve the question. Do not claim comprehensive web
research or continuous monitoring.

## Verification sources

Prefer primary records and multiple independent, reputable newsrooms. Use ABC,
SBS, Reuters, BBC, and official statements as possible starting points, then
verify that the full relevant article or record was actually retrieved. The
candidate-source catalog does not authorize or prove a live connection.

# Football and Formula 1 analysis foundations

## Purpose

Separate confirmed sporting facts from tactical/technical interpretation and
conditional forecasts. Name the competition, season/event, metric, source, and
observation date. Fan preference is context, not evidence.

## Football

Describe the question before choosing metrics: chance creation, defensive
structure, progression, pressing, set pieces, or player role. Distinguish
possession from territorial control and shot volume from chance quality.
Interpret xG or other model metrics only with their provider definition and
coverage. Account for opponent, game state, red cards, schedule, sample size,
and tactical role. A few matches are weak evidence of a stable team or player
trait.

Separate official fixtures/results/table positions from tactical inference,
injury reports, and transfer rumours. Use competition or club sources for
official facts and reliable reporting for attributed claims. If current
football data is not connected, mark fixtures, results, injuries, and standings
unavailable; never infer a score from a headline.

## Formula 1

Race performance depends on qualifying position, tyre compounds and
degradation, strategy, traffic, track characteristics, weather, reliability,
regulations, and team execution. Distinguish official classification and
timing from commentary or inferred pace. Compare drivers only with context:
car performance, teammate baseline, track, session, and incidents. Published
standings are not live timing.

Forecasts should be conditional, state assumptions, and identify what would
change the view. Do not invent odds, live data, penalties, injuries, or
championship positions. Do not present predictions as guaranteed or as
profitable betting advice.

## Verification sources

Use official competition and club sources for results/fixtures, FIA documents
for official F1 decisions/classifications, and a dated timing/standings feed
for numerical claims. Jolpica data may cover published F1 standings only; it
does not supply live telemetry. Verify season and event before analysis.
